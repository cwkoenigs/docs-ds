```markdown
tags: [architecture]
---

# ADR-NNN: [Decision title]

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: [People or team]

## Context

[What problem or constraint requires a decision?]

## Decision

[What will we do, and why?]

## Alternatives considered

[Which alternatives were evaluated, and why were they set aside?]

## Consequences

[Benefits, costs, risks, and follow-up work.]

## References

[Links to related issues, projects, and decisions.]
```
