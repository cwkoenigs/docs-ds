The portal footer: one line of small muted text on a near-black navy band. Hand-written from `src/css/custom.css` as a static rendition; the portal renders it with Docusaurus's dark footer.

## When to use

At the bottom of every portal page, in both themes.

## What the consumer provides

- The copyright line (`Data Science Team`). Nothing else: no link columns, no logo.

## Rules

- Ground `#001126` and text `#c0cdd5` (11.7:1) are literals in the stylesheet, not tokens; the same in both themes.
- Padding 1.4rem; text `footer` (0.875rem).
- Square corners, no rule above, no shadow.
